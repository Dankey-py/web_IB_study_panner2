Behind

A planning tool is also a series of decisions.

How should work be stored? What should AI be allowed to change? What does a study-time summary really tell us? These questions shape the system.

01THE DESIGN BRIEF

Connect the work.
Keep it editable.

The product is built around three practical requirements.

  1. Give work a context. Connect tasks to subjects, priorities and deadlines.
  2. Distinguish intention from activity. Keep planned work and logged study sessions as different records.
  3. Make changes reviewable. Let students inspect AI proposals before saving them.

The IB context brings subject levels and long coursework projects into the workflow. Much of the underlying task and calendar functionality also applies to other study programmes.

04DECISIONS & TRADE-OFFS

01

Why require AI review?

+

The decision: Keep proposed tasks and project phases editable until confirmation.

The trade-off: Review adds a step, but makes it possible to correct a misunderstood deadline or unrealistic plan before saving.

The boundary: Confirmation gives control over a change; it does not guarantee that the plan is academically sound.

02

Why show a simple priority score?

+

The decision: Suggest a task order using selected priority and deadline urgency.

The trade-off: A visible rule is easier to explain, but cannot account for every task’s difficulty, emotional cost or importance.

The boundary: This is rule-based ranking. It is not a personalised AI scheduling model.

03

Why keep planned and completed work separate?

+

The decision: Store tasks and recorded study sessions separately, with links to subjects.

The trade-off: Students need to record activity. A calendar alone cannot show how long work actually took.

The boundary: Logged time and completion show recorded activity, rather than the quality of learning.

05BUILDING PLAN

What exists.
What comes next.

Implementation stages describe the current project structure. Future directions are proposals, not promises or completed features.

  1. 01
    IMPLEMENTED

    Establish the foundations

    Accounts, profiles, IB subjects and persistent records.

  2. 02
    IMPLEMENTED

    Connect planning and activity

    Task editing, monthly calendar, focus timer and study summaries.

  3. 03
    IMPLEMENTED

    Add reviewed AI proposals

    Contextual conversation, editable task changes and long-term project phases.

  4. 04
    PROPOSED / EVALUATION

    Test whether the workflow is clear

    Ask students to plan a sample project. Observe where they hesitate, what they edit and whether they understand the confirmation step.

  5. 05
    PROPOSED / REFINEMENT

    Improve the plan using evidence

    Use observed difficulties to refine guidance, date handling and mobile review. Recheck the same tasks after each change.

“Implemented” describes features found in the local source reviewed for this showcase. It does not claim that every external service has been tested or that the product has demonstrated learning outcomes.
