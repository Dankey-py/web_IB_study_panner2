Future
development.

StudyOS can organise a workload, but an organised calendar does not necessarily make the work manageable. I want to understand what happens when other students use the planner and find that its suggestions do not fit their week.

I would begin with a small group of IB students planning a coursework project. Rather than only asking whether they like the interface, I would observe which tasks they change and ask why. Their explanations could reveal assumptions that I have built into the system without noticing.

Those observations would guide the next changes: helping students recognise competing deadlines and reconsider unrealistic time estimates. I would also explore editable structures for longer IB projects, while leaving room for different subjects and school requirements.

The aim is to develop a planner that students can question and adjust. I want its development to follow the difficulties they encounter, including those I did not anticipate.
