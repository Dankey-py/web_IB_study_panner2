02THE PROBLEM

Knowing what is due
is only the beginning.

Revision, coursework and long projects compete for the same hours.

A list records the workload. It leaves another question open: what should happen next? StudyOS connects tasks to subjects, places work on a calendar, and records the time actually spent studying.

The design question is simple: how can planning become clearer while the student stays in control?
