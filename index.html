02The idea

Why I put these
functions together

IB work includes revision, coursework and longer projects. Recording a deadline is useful, but it does not explain what to do in each study session.

The idea of this project is to connect the overall plan with smaller tasks. A task belongs to a subject, can have a deadline, and can appear on the calendar. Study sessions record the time actually spent on the work.

I also added Danky, an AI assistant. It uses information from the planner to suggest tasks. The student checks the suggestion before it is saved.
