02The idea

What needs to happen
before the deadline?

For a Physics report due on Friday, the deadline gives the work an end point. It still leaves a practical question: what should I do in the next study session?

I organised StudyOS around that question. A subject gives the work a context. A task gives it a specific action, and the calendar gives that action a place in the week.

Danky can help break a project into phases. I kept a review step because a suggested plan still needs to fit the student’s workload. The dates and tasks can be changed before they reach the calendar.

03A simple example

Breaking down a project
