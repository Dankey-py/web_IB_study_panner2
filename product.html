01Subjects and tasks

Subjects, then tasks

I can add an IB subject, its level and a target grade. Tasks can then be linked to that subject.

Each task has a title, priority and status. It can also have a deadline and scheduled study time.

02Danky — the AI assistant

Checking a plan from Danky

Danky uses saved subjects, goals and plans as context. For a longer project, it can suggest several phases. I kept a review step so the student can change the suggestion before saving.

03Study time and progress

Recording study time

A focus timer

Start a study session for a subject. There is also a form for adding study time after finishing the work.

A record of study activity

The overview shows recorded time, weekly activity and completed tasks. This helps the student compare attention across subjects.

A limit I need to keep clear

Less recorded time does not automatically mean a student is weak in a subject. These records show activity; they do not measure understanding or predict a grade.
