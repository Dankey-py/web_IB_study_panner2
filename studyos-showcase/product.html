04STUDY & REFLECT

Record what
actually happened.

01 / FOCUS

Start with a session.

Use the focus timer or log a completed session manually. Link the session to its subject to keep a record of time spent.

02 / REVIEW ACTIVITY

Look beyond the to-do list.

See recorded study time, weekly activity and task completion. Compare attention across subjects to decide what to revisit.

03 / INTERPRET CAREFULLY

Time spent is one signal.

A subject with less logged time may deserve attention. The indicator cannot measure understanding or predict a grade.
